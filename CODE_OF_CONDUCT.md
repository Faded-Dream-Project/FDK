# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. The FDK project's tracker exists to make a toolkit
better, and everyone in it is here voluntarily. Rudeness,
harassment, and hostility drive people away and make the software
worse.

## What applies

This code of conduct applies to the project's GitHub tracker
(issues, pull requests, discussions), and to any other space the
project owner names as covered.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith in reports and replies; most friction is
  missing context, not malice.
- Stay on the technical topic. Bug reports, feature requests, and
  review comments should be about the code and the behavior, not
  the person.
- Be precise and honest — including about what you do not know
  and what you have not tested. False "it's fixed" claims waste
  more of everyone's time than admitting a problem.
- Accept that "no" and "not now" are legitimate answers,
  especially from a small project with a proprietary development
  posture (see `CONTRIBUTING.md` for what contribution means
  today).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about
  anyone, including on the basis of identity.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption: thread hijacking, bad-faith arguing,
  repeated re-filing of rejected issues.
- Any behavior that would be considered unacceptable in a
  professional workplace.

## Reporting

Report violations to the project owner via the repository's
contact channel (a private GitHub mention or an issue marked as a
conduct report). Reports are handled by the project owner, who
decides the response. The project is small; responses may be
slower than a large community's, but every report is read.

## Consequences

The project owner may close issues, block accounts, or otherwise
limit participation for violations. The goal is keeping the space
usable, not punishment; warnings precede escalation where the
violation is not severe.

## Scope note

This document governs the project's own spaces. It does not
change anything about the software's license (`LICENSE`) or the
contribution policy (`CONTRIBUTING.md`).
